# Granth catalogue

What granths and Gurmukhi (or adjacent) texts we have, what we're looking for, and every
version of each. It sits beside the Drive inventory, which says what each *file* is
technically (text layer, fonts); the catalogue says what each file *is* bibliographically,
and also records copies that are not in Drive yet.

- `catalogue.json`: the truth. Edit it by hand or with `candidates.py`.
- `CATALOGUE.md`, `views/*.csv`: generated by `build.py`. Don't edit.
- `SWEEP.md`: the daily sweep's runbook.
- `data/sweeps.jsonl`: one line per sweep run.

## Model

**Work**: a text (Sri Gur Sobha, Lav Kus di Var).
`id` (`w-…`), `title` {`roman`, `gurmukhi`, `variants`}, `authors` [{`name`, `note`}],
`date` (composition), `status`, `known_from`, `refs` (e.g. Anandpur list number), `notes`.

**Version**: one witness of one or more works.
`id` (`v-…`), `label` (the citation), `type`, `contains` [{`work_id`, `locator`, `note`}],
`scripts`, `languages`, `date`, `contributors` (scribe/editor), `publisher`, `pages`,
`repository`, `shelfmark`, `host`, `availability`, `item_url`, `file_url`, `status`,
`drive_id`, `drive_title`, `drive_folder`, `text_layer`, `machine_readable`,
`found_on`, `found_by`, `user_notes`.

- `type`: manuscript, lithograph, early-print (pre-1947), edition, e-text, translation,
  commentary, compilation (gutka, nitnem), anthology (carries extracts), study,
  catalogue-record (known only from a catalogue), reference (a catalogue or encyclopaedia).
- Dates are `{text, from, to, approx}` in CE; `text` keeps what the source says. A work's
  `date` is when it was composed; a version's `date` is when that copy was made or printed.
  `schema.parse_date` reads years, ranges, "c. 1800", "18th century", Gurmukhi and
  Devanagari numerals, and Bikrami (Sammat/Samvat/Bk/BS/VS, ਸੰਮਤ, संवत्: −57, approx unless
  the CE year is also given). Nanakshahi and Khalsa Samvat years are left alone. Validation
  refuses a Bikrami date stored unconverted.
- **Era** (computed): pre-1850 (prime interest), 1850-1899 (strong), 1900+, undated; from the
  composition date, else the earliest copy. The wanted list and the sweeps go in that order.
- `scripts`: Gurmukhi, Devanagari, Perso-Arabic, Roman, Sharada, Takri, Landa, Other.
- Work `status`: catalogued, needs-curation, candidate, rejected.
  Version `status`: in-drive (has `drive_id`), available, catalogue-only, candidate, rejected.
  Rejected rows stay, so sweeps never propose them again.

**Holding** is computed per work, never stored: historic (a manuscript, lithograph or early
print in Drive) > edition > extracts > online-only > known-only > nothing. Every work short of
historic is on the wanted list.

## Ashok's manuscript catalogues (2026-10-29)

Shamsher Singh Ashok's *Sikh Reference Library manuscript catalogue* (SGPC 1968) and *Panjabi
Hath Likhtan di Suchi* vol. 2 (Bhasha Vibhag 1963) describe hundreds of manuscripts, many of them
lost since (most of the Sikh Reference Library in 1984). Both were OCR'd (tesseract, Panjabi,
300 dpi); the text sits beside each PDF in Drive as `<PDF name> - OCR text.txt`.

`ashok.py parse` reads each entry: title, MS number, holder (vol. 2), author, translator,
folios, composition date (ਰਚਨਾ ਕਾਲ), copy date (ਨਕਲ, or a lone ਸਮਾਂ), place, scribe, and the
texts in a multi-text MS. `data/ashok/*.jsonl` keeps one entry per line; the catalogues' prose
and incipit/explicit quotations are not kept. `ashok.py import` adds each entry as a
`catalogue-record` candidate version; its texts are matched to known works by title skeleton
(`match_key`), else proposed as new works with Gurmukhi and roman titles.

OCR caveats, handled conservatively: a tippi misread in a title is restored only where the
Gurbani lexicon knows the corrected word (`fix_title`); a five-digit year is OCR noise, so its
date goes into a note; vol. 2's holder abbreviations come from its key (`HOLDERS`), and six from
vol. 1's key, not in Drive, are inferred and say so in their notes (`HOLDERS_INFERRED`). The
label keeps the OCR'd title and every note gives the PDF page, so any reading can be checked.

## Curating

Curation is a list of operations in `data/curation/<date>-<topic>.json`, applied all or
nothing by `curate.py` (merge, new_work, update_work, move_version, add_to_version,
update_version, reject; see its docstring). Keeping the ops as files makes each decision
reviewable in the PR and repeatable. Conventions:
- A steek, translation or study is a work of its own, linked by `based_on` to the work it
  is on. Its versions' `type` is their physical form (a handwritten teeka is a manuscript).
- An extract of a larger granth (a Sarbloh Manglacharan, Chandi Charitar II) is a version
  of that granth with a `locator`, not a separate work.
- Teaching materials and guides are rejected, not deleted.
